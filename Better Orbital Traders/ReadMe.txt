BETTER ORBITAL TRADERS
======================
RimWorld mod by Coldcrow
Workshop: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3009866854
Source:   https://github.com/Coldcrow69/Better-Orbital-Traders


DESCRIPTION
-----------
Better Orbital Traders is a powerful re-balance of vanilla orbital traders plus eight new
orbital trader types, including an orbital scrapyard that buys ANYTHING. Get rid of your
junk!

Base game orbital traders carry far more silver (within reason) and their stock has been
re-balanced to match the size of a ship and the amount of goods it should carry. Late
game and wealthy colonies get a trade partner that can keep up, and early game colonies
get more options.

Features:
- Much more silver on every base game (and DLC) orbital trader
- Re-balanced base game trader stock: the bulk goods ship carries every textile and raw
  resource by the thousand, gold and jade included, and other ships get tweaked items and
  quantities
- Orbital scrapyard: buys ALL of your items
- Orbital ecosphere: plant and animal products, livestock, pets, uncommon wildlife, rare
  seeds
- Organ farm: buys and sells humans and human accessories (organs and prosthetics), plus
  genepacks, archite capsules, hemogen, and deathrest serum with Biotech
- Narcotics dealer: smuggles every kind of drug and medicine down from wealthy
  glitterworlds
- Autonomous archive: books, modded ones included, and techprints, plus tomes
  (Anomaly) and maps (Odyssey)
- Orbital shipbreaker (Odyssey): gravlite panels, vacstone, obsidian, shuttle engines,
  components, and bulk metals. Buys gravship upgrades
- Void peddler (Anomaly): bioferrite, shards, tomes, dread leather, serums, cultist gear,
  deadlife shells. Buys revenant spines, and void sculptures with Better Tradable Items
- Guild flagship (Odyssey): never arrives on its own. Request it from the comms console as
  a Guild ally for more silver than any other ship and a slice of every other ship's stock
- Odyssey: all eight ships belong to the Traders Guild, like the vanilla orbital
  traders. Request them from the comms console (allied, costs goodwill, has a
  cooldown), trade and gift with them for Guild goodwill, and expect them to stay
  away while the Guild is hostile
- Combat Extended: the flagship stocks CE ammo, FSX, prometheum, and turrets like CE's
  combat supplier, the shipbreaker salvages turrets, heavy ammo, and FSX, and both buy ammo
  and turrets back

This mod does not touch ground traders or traders added by other mods. Only base game
(and DLC) orbital traders, and the ones this mod adds.


REQUIREMENTS
------------
- RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7.
- No DLC required. The imperial trader is patched only when Royalty is active, the
  Biotech goods on the organ farm only show up with Biotech, the void peddler only exists
  with Anomaly, and the Traders Guild integration, the shipbreaker, and the flagship only
  exist with Odyssey.


INSTALLATION
------------
Steam Workshop: subscribe on the page linked above, then enable the mod in the mod list.
Manual: copy the "Better Orbital Traders" folder into RimWorld/Mods and enable it. Load
order does not matter.


COMPATIBILITY
-------------
XML only. Works with anything that doesn't also change base game orbital trader stock. If
another mod does, whichever loads last wins and you may get odd stock. Nothing in here
can crash the game.

Mods that add new traders or new trading mechanics are fine as long as they leave the
base game orbital trader stock alone, which most do.

Tested alongside Call A Trader by Hau-Drauf-Klaus. Trader Ships by AUTOMATIC hasn't been
re-tested since the ships joined the Traders Guild, so no promises there. Call A Trader
lists every orbital ship, flagship included, so it can summon the flagship without the
Guild's goodwill cost or cooldown. That's its call, not this mod's.

Combat Extended: any load order. CE removes vanilla mortar shells from every trader and
adds its own ammo; this mod's mortar shell change on the combat supplier skips itself when
the line is already gone. On 1.x this mod had to load before CE or you got four red errors
at startup. With CE active the flagship stocks CE ammo, FSX, prometheum, and turrets at the
combat supplier's scale and up, and the shipbreaker salvages turrets, heavy ammo, and FSX.
Both buy ammo and turrets back. The scrapyard buys them like everything else.

Trading Options Continued: works on top of this mod, and its multipliers stack with these
numbers, so a 2x silver slider on a 50,000 silver ship is 100,000.


FAQ
---
Q: Can this be added mid-save?
A: Yes.

Q: Can this be removed mid-save?
A: Yes. Make sure none of the modded trade ships are over your map, then uninstall.

Q: Do I need the DLCs to use this mod?
A: No.

Q: Red errors at startup about MortarShell on the combat supplier, with Combat Extended?
A: Fixed in 2.0, any load order works. On 1.x, load this mod before Combat Extended.

Q: The Traders Guild hates me and your ships stopped showing up. Bug?
A: No. With Odyssey the ships belong to the Guild, and the game doesn't send ships from a
   hostile faction. Same as the vanilla orbital traders. Fix your standing and they come
   back.

Q: Does the scrapyard take toxic wastepacks?
A: With Better Tradable Items 2.0, yes, a silver per hundred. The scrapyard buys anything
   the game lets you sell at all. Vanilla gives wastepacks no value, and marks the gravcore,
   mechanoid transponder, and void sculpture untradeable outright, so without that mod none
   of them is ever listed.

Q: Can I get sliders for silver and stock?
A: Not from an XML mod. Trading Options Continued works on top of this one; its multipliers
   stack with these numbers, so set them with that in mind.

Q: Where is thrumbomane?
A: It needs Better Tradable Items, which makes it buyable. The bulk goods ship then stocks
   it by the thousand, like every other textile it carries.

Q: Do the ships carry modded items?
A: Yes. The drug, organ, prosthetic, and book lines pull from categories, so modded drugs,
   prosthetics (EPOE included), and books show up on their own. Other mods' traders are
   left alone.

Q: Multiplayer?
A: Untested. XML only, so nothing in here should desync.

Q: Can I run this with the standalone Orbital Scrapyard mod?
A: Don't. It's the same ship; running both gives you two of it at best.

Q: Is this mod compatible with another mod?
A: Probably. This is an XML only mod, so the worst behavior that you should get is
   improper trade items on base game orbital traders. It's not likely to cause any
   crashes, and as long as the other mod doesn't modify base game orbital traders, it's
   unlikely there will be any conflict. See the compatibility notes above.

Q: I have a bug, and I think it's caused by this mod. What do?
A: Post a bug report on the Workshop page.

Q: Does it work on the 1.7 unstable branch?
A: Yes. 1.7 moved RimWorld to Ludeon's own Waylith engine, but the defs this mod patches
   did not change, so 1.6 and 1.7 load the same files.

Q: RimWorld updated, but this mod hasn't yet. What do?
A: Life happens sometimes, but I will do my best to update all of my mods, including
   this one, as quickly as I can.

Q: I want to use your mod or pieces of your mod in mine, is that okay?
A: Yes. See LICENSE.txt (MIT), and credit me in your mod's documentation and on its
   Workshop page.

CHANGELOG

2.0
- Requires RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7. Support for 1.4 and 1.5 dropped. 1.7 replaced the game
  engine, not the defs this mod touches, so both versions load the same files.
- Combat Extended: no load order needed any more. CE deletes every vanilla mortar shell
  stock line, and when it loaded first this mod's two mortar shell edits on the combat
  supplier failed with red errors. They now skip when the line is gone. With CE active the
  flagship stocks CE ammo, FSX, prometheum, and turrets, the shipbreaker salvages turrets,
  heavy ammo, and FSX, and both buy ammo and turrets back.
- New ship with Odyssey: the orbital shipbreaker. Gravlite panels, vacstone blocks,
  obsidian, shuttle engines, components, and bulk steel, plasteel, uranium, and
  chemfuel. Buys gravship upgrades. Requestable from the Guild like the rest.
- New ship with Anomaly: the void peddler. Bioferrite, shards, tomes, dread leather, the
  Anomaly serums at the narcotics dealer's caps, cultist masks, deadlife and disruptor
  packs, deadlife shells. Buys revenant spines, Horax gear, and void sculptures (vanilla
  marks those untradeable; Better Tradable Items makes them sellable, and without it the
  buy line does nothing).
- New ship with Odyssey: the Guild flagship. Commonality 0, so it never arrives on its
  own; allied players request it from the comms console. 200,000 to 300,000 silver,
  more than any other ship, and a slice of every other ship's stock: metals and
  components, spacer medicine, weapons and armor, implants, art, artifacts, books,
  techprints, gravship parts.
- Ecosphere: now sells uncommon wildlife too (most of Odyssey's new animals), buys fish
  (Odyssey), and carries sentience catalysts and archean seeds (Odyssey), Gauranlen
  seeds (Ideology), and polux seeds (Biotech). Fertilized eggs removed from stock, same
  rule as vanilla food traders. Animals: 4 to 8 kinds and 25 to 50 head in total (vanilla's
  bulk goods ship tops out at 20; 1.x rolled 10 to 20 kinds and 10 to 20 head), and farm
  animals come as a breeding pair like vanilla's. Kibble dropped from its stock: at Better
  Kibble's price it was near-free food.
- Archive: tomes (Anomaly), maps (Odyssey), and books from other mods (3 to 6 kinds at
  1 to 2 each) next to the vanilla titles. Techprints 4, 6, or 8 per visit (was always
  10).
- Archive and flagship carry fewer books. Every generated book builds a full author pawn,
  so book count is arrival cost: the 1.x archive caused lag spikes and, next to mods that
  patch pawn generation, failed arrivals. Now 2 to 4 of each vanilla kind on the archive
  (8 to 18 books a visit, still double any vanilla ship) and 1 to 2 on the flagship.
- Organ farm: basic and standard subcores (Biotech). No longer sells psylink
  neuroformers or ghoul implants. Natural organs 5 to 15 each (was 10 to 25). Gene trade
  with Biotech: genepacks 15 to 30 (was 5 to 10), archite capsules 2 to 4 (was 2),
  hemogen 500 to 1,000 (was 100 to 500), deathrest capacity serum 1 to 2 (new).
- Narcotics dealer: rare drugs capped. Luciferium 25 to 75, deathrest capacity serum
  1 to 3, juggernaut and metalblood serum 2 to 5, mind-numb and voidsight serum 3 to 8.
  Common drugs stay at 100 to 500. Sentience catalyst moved to the ecosphere. Healer mech
  serum 1 to 2, resurrector 0 to 1, and ghoul resurrection serum 2 to 5 are capped too,
  for anyone running Better Tradable Items 2.0, which makes two of them buyable.
- Bulk goods trader: spacer components 10 to 25 (was 25 to 100), so the exotic trader
  stays the spacer-tech source. Thrumbomane rides its textile line by the thousand like
  every other textile, for anyone running Better Tradable Items 2.0, which makes it buyable
  (Odyssey). Imperial trader silver 100,000 to 150,000 (was 100,000 to 200,000).
- Odyssey: all ships now belong to the Traders Guild, same as the vanilla orbital
  traders. They can be requested from the comms console (allied, goodwill cost,
  cooldown), trading and gifting with them moves Guild goodwill, and they stop coming
  while the Guild is hostile. No effect without Odyssey.
- Files moved to Common/, loaded through LoadFolders.xml.
- Removed three "buy" entries that pointed at trade tags which do not exist
  (PlantFoodRaw, PlantMatter, BodyParts). The category entries on those ships already
  buy the same goods, so nothing changes in game.
- Biotech MayRequire id normalized to Ludeon.RimWorld.Biotech. The game compares ids

- Core patch: remove-then-add pairs collapsed into single replace ops, and xpaths
